# Stickers

Copy stickers to your own packs or download sticker and GIF files.

## User commands

- `/stickerid`: Reply to a sticker to get its file ID.
- `/gifid`: Reply to a GIF to get its file ID.
- `/getsticker`: Reply to a static sticker to download it as PNG.
- `/getvideo`: Reply to a GIF to download its video file.
- `/getvidsticker`: Reply to a video sticker to download its video file. TGS animation stickers are not supported here.
- `/kang [pack_number] [emoji]`: Reply to supported sticker or image media to add it to your pack.
- `/unkang`: Reply to a sticker from your pack to remove it. Alias: `/delsticker`.
- `/pkang [pack_title]`: Reply to a sticker in a pack to copy that pack. Alias: `/packkang`.

## Example

Reply to the sticker or image to add:

```
/kang 2 😀
```
