# Stickers

I can copy stickers into your packs, copy a whole pack, or download sticker and GIF files.

Replace `<...>` with required input; `[...]` is optional. Do not type those brackets.

**User commands:**

- `/stickerid`: Reply to a sticker to get its file ID.
- `/gifid`: Reply to a GIF to get its file ID.
- `/getsticker`: Reply to a static sticker to download it as a PNG.
- `/getvideo`: Reply to a GIF to download its video file.
- `/getvidsticker`: Reply to a video sticker to download its video file. TGS animated stickers are not supported by this command.
- `/kang [pack_number] [emoji]`: Reply to a supported sticker or image to add it to your pack.
- `/unkang`: Reply to a sticker from your pack to remove it. Alias: `/delsticker`.
- `/pkang [pack_title]`: Reply to a sticker to copy its entire pack. Alias: `/packkang`.

**Examples:**

- Add a sticker or image to pack 2. Reply to it:\
  `/kang 2 😀`
- Add an image using its URL:\
  `/kang https://example.com/image.png 2 😀`
