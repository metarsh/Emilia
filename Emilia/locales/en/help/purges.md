# Purges

Need to clear a run of unwanted messages? I can delete a range for you, or just one message.

Use these commands by reply in the group. I need Delete messages rights. Deleted messages cannot be restored by me.

**Admin commands:**

- `/purge`: Reply to the first unwanted message to delete everything from there through your command.
- `/spurge`: Delete the same range without sending a confirmation message.
- `/del`: Reply to a message to delete it and your command.
- `/purgefrom`: Reply to the first message of a range to mark its start.
- `/purgeto`: Reply to the last message to delete the marked range, including the first and last messages.

**Examples:**

- Delete from an unwanted message through your command. Reply to that message:\
  `/purge`
- Delete one message. Reply to it:\
  `/del`
- Delete a specific range. Reply to its first message:\
  `/purgefrom`\
  Then reply to its last message:\
  `/purgeto`
