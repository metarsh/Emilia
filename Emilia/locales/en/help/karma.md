# Karma

You can give members Karma points by replying to their messages. This is separate from Levels' XP and reputation.

Replace `<...>` with required input; `[...]` is optional. Do not type those brackets.

**Admin commands:**

- `/karma <enable|disable>`: Turn karma on or off.

Karma is off until an admin enables it. A vote counts only when your entire reply is one of the listed words or symbols; capitalisation does not matter. Each vote changes the member's score by one point, and I show their new total. You cannot vote for yourself.

**Voting:**

- Upvote: `+`, `++`, `+1`, `thx`, `tnx`, `ty`, `thank you`, `thanx`, `thanks`, `pro`, `cool`, `good`, `👍`, `nice`, `noice`, `piro`
- Downvote: `-`, `--`, `-1`, `👎`, `noob`, `gross`, `fuck off`

**Example:**

- Give a member an upvote. Reply to their message:\
  `+1`
