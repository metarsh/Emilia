# AniList searches

I can search AniList for anime, manga, characters, and studios. AniList is an anime and manga catalogue. Start with a title or name; supported AniList IDs also work.

**User commands:**

- `/anime <query>`: View information about an anime.
- `/anilist <query>`: Browse matching anime.
- `/manga <query>`: Browse matching manga.
- `/character <query>`: Browse matching characters.
- `/studio <query>`: Browse matching studios.
- `/airing <query>`: Check an anime's airing information.
- `/user <username>`: Look up an AniList username, which may differ from the person's Telegram username.
- `/top [genre or tag]`: Browse top anime, with an optional genre or tag.
- `/gettags [nsfw]`: List tags.
- `/getgenres`: List available genres.
- `/browse`: Browse popular, trending, and upcoming anime.

**Examples:**

- Look up an anime:\
  `/anime Frieren`
- Browse top fantasy anime:\
  `/top fantasy`
