# AniList account

Authorize your AniList account to manage favorites, activity, and lists.

## User commands

- `/auth`: Start AniList authorization.
- `/code <authorization code>`: Complete manual authorization in private chat.
- `/flex`: Display your authorized AniList profile.
- `/activity`: View recent AniList activity. Alias: `/ame`.
- `/favourites`: Browse saved AniList favorites.
- `/logout`: Disconnect AniList account in private chat.
