# AniList account

Authorize your AniList account to view activity, manage favorites, and update list entries through search buttons.

## User commands

- `/auth`: Open the AniList authorization flow.
- `/code <authorization code>`: Complete manual authorization in a private chat.
- `/flex`: Show your authorized AniList profile.
- `/activity`: Show your recent AniList activity. Alias: `/ame`.
- `/favourites`: Browse your AniList favorites.
- `/logout`: Disconnect your AniList account in private.
