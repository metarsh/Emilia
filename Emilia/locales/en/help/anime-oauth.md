# AniList account

Connect your AniList account to view your profile, activity, and favourites. You can also update your list entries through search buttons.

**User commands:**

- `/auth`: Open the AniList account connection steps.
- `/code <authorization code>`: Complete a manual connection in private using the authorization code from the `/auth` steps.
- `/flex`: View your connected AniList profile.
- `/activity`: View your recent AniList activity. Alias: `/ame`.
- `/favourites`: Browse your AniList favourites.
- `/logout`: Disconnect your AniList account in private.

**Examples:**

- Start connecting your account:\
  `/auth`
- View your profile after connecting:\
  `/flex`
