# Music

Stream audio and video in group voice chats. Start voice chat first.

## User commands

- `/play [query or link]`: Stream audio by query, link, or reply.
- `/vplay [query or link]`: Stream video by query, link, or reply.
- `/now`: Show current track and controls.
- `/queue`: View upcoming queue.
- `/lyrics <song>`: Search song lyrics.

## Playback controls

- `/pause`: Pause playback.
- `/resume`: Resume playback.
- `/skip`: Skip to next track.
- `/end`: Stop playback and clear queue. Alias: `/cend`.
- `/remove <position>`: Remove track position from queue.
- `/clearqueue`: Empty upcoming queue.
- `/loop <off|one|all>`: Loop current track or entire queue.
- `/shuffle <on|off>`: Shuffle queue order.

## Admin commands

- `/autoplay [on|off]`: Auto-play recommended tracks when queue ends. Alias: `/cplay`.
- `/playmenu [on|off]`: Toggle search selection menu.

## Example

```
/play Never Gonna Give You Up
```
