# Levels

I can track group activity as experience points (XP) and show your rank. Register a profile first to claim bonuses and rewards.

Replace `<...>` with required input; `[...]` is optional. Do not type those brackets.

**User commands:**

- `/register <name>`: Create a profile with a unique name of up to 20 characters.
- `/rank`: View your rank card and progress.
- `/leaderboard`: Open the group leaderboard. Use its buttons for other views.
- `/daily`: Claim a daily XP bonus. Consecutive daily claims build a streak.
- `/weekly`: Claim a weekly XP bonus when its cooldown ends.
- `/rankings`: Read rank names and requirements in private.
- `/prestige`: At the maximum rank, reset your level for a prestige badge and XP multiplier.
- `/thanks`: Reply to another member to give them reputation, once per 24 hours in this group. Alias: `/rep`.

**Admin commands:**

- `/level [on|off]`: View or change whether levels are enabled.
- `/levelset event <multiplier>`: Set the group's event XP multiplier from 0.1 to 100.

XP advances your rank. Reputation from `/thanks` is a separate score, and Karma uses its own upvote/downvote replies. The Karma guide below explains those votes.

**Examples:**

- Create your profile:\
  `/register EmiliaFan`
- View your progress:\
  `/rank`

Use the Karma guide below for respect points.
