# Lock examples

Choose lock names exactly as they appear in `/locktypes`.

**Examples:**

- Lock photos, videos, and GIFs:\
  `/lock photo video gif`
- Allow photos and videos again:\
  `/unlock photo video`
- Lock website links and invite links:\
  `/lock url invitelink`
- Allow a trusted website:\
  `/allowlist example.com`
- Remove that website from the allowlist:\
  `/rmallowlist example.com`
- Lock custom emoji:\
  `/lock emojicustom`
- Allow a custom emoji pack. Reply to its emoji and type `<>` literally:\
  `/allowlist emojipack:<>`
- Lock member reactions and reactions from people outside the group:\
  `/lock reaction outsidereaction`
