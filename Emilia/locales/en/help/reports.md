# Reports

See a message that needs an admin's attention? Reply to it and I can report it for review.

I reply in the group and mention its human admins so they can review the message. Reports are enabled by default.

This reporting command is for ordinary members. It cannot report admin messages or messages sent as a channel.

Replace `<...>` with required input; `[...]` is optional. Do not type those brackets.

**User commands:**

- `/report`: Reply to a message to report it. You can also mention @admin or @admins in a reply.

**Admin commands:**

- `/reports [on|off]`: View or change whether members may report messages. You need Change group info rights.

**Examples:**

- Report a message. Reply to it:\
  `/report`
- Allow members to send reports:\
  `/reports on`
