# Fun

Ask me for a game, a GIF, or a joke. You can also mark yourself as away or turn text into speech.

Replace `<...>` with required input; `[...]` is optional. Do not type those brackets.

**User commands:**

- `/pokedex <name>`: Look up a Pokémon.
- `/joke`: Get a random joke. Aliases: `/jokes`, `/funny`.
- `/couples`: Pick a daily couple in the group.
- `/gif <query> [; count]`: Search for GIFs. Get one by default, or choose a count from 1 to 20.
- `/react`: Add a random reaction to your message or the message you reply to.
- `/truth`: Get a truth question.
- `/dare`: Get a dare challenge.
- `/afk [reason]`: Mark yourself as away. When someone replies to you or starts a group message with a mention of you, I show how long you have been away and your reason. Send another message to return.

**Text to speech:**

- `/tts <language> <text>`: Turn text into speech using a language code such as `en` or `ja`.
- `/tts [language]`: Reply to text or a caption to read it aloud. The default language is `en`; a single inline word also uses `en`.

**AFK shortcuts:**

Messages starting with `+afk`, `!afk`, `?afk`, `.afk`, `brb`, or `i go away` also mark you away.

**Examples:**

- Get 5 cat GIFs:\
  `/gif cats ; 5`
- Read a message aloud in English:\
  `/tts en Hello there`
