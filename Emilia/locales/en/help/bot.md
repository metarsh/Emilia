# Bot commands

I can help you manage a group, answer questions, and use the features in this help menu.

**Get started in a group:**

- Add me through your group's Add members screen, then make me an admin.
- Enable the rights you need: Delete messages for cleanup, Ban users for moderation, Pin messages for pins, and Add new admins for promotions. Topic commands need Manage topics; group-setting changes need Change group info.
- Start with `/help greetings` for welcome messages and `/help rules` for group rules.
- To manage settings in private, open `/help connections`.

Replace `<...>` with required input; `[...]` is optional. Do not type those brackets.

**User commands:**

- `/start`: Open my main menu.
- `/build`: View Hanabi's separate service for building websites, apps, custom bots, and AI workflows.
- `/help [module]`: Open help, or open a guide by its title or short name, such as `notes`.
- `/ping`: Check response time and how long I have been running.
- `/donate`: View donation information.
- `/feedback`: Find this bot's owner's contact information in private.

**Example:**

- Open the notes guide:\
  `/help notes`
