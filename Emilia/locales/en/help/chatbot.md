# Chatbot

I can have a conversation when you reply to one of my messages. I do not answer every group message.

This feature works through the main Emilia bot and is off in a group until an admin enables it.

Replace `<...>` with required input; `[...]` is optional. Do not type those brackets.

**User commands:**

- `/reset`: Clear the saved conversation for your user account so you can start fresh. This does not delete messages already sent in Telegram.

**Admin commands:**

- `/chatbot <on|off>`: Turn conversational replies on or off in the group.

**Example:**

- Enable group conversations:\
  `/chatbot on`
- Then reply to one of my messages with your question. Continue by replying to my answer.

Use ordinary text in your reply. Bot commands and messages containing mentions do not start a chatbot answer.
