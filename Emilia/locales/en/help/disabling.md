# Disabling

I can turn off selected commands in your group or channel, including anime commands. Admins can still use them.

You can change these settings in the chat or through a connected private chat.

Replace `<...>` with required input; `[...]` is optional. Do not type those brackets.

**Admin commands:**

- `/disableable`: List the commands you can disable.
- `/disable <command> [command ...]`: Turn off one or more command names.
- `/enable <command> [command ...]`: Turn them on again.
- `/disabled`: List disabled commands.
- `/disabledel <on|off>`: Choose whether attempts to use disabled commands are deleted.

**Examples:**

- Turn off Wikipedia searches and GIF searches:\
  `/disable wiki gif`
- Turn them on again:\
  `/enable wiki gif`

**Tips:**

- Use names from `/disableable`.
- To disable a listed alias group such as `pkang|packkang` together, type the `|` literally. You can also disable an individual alias.
- Command deletion applies to messages in the selected chat. Commands sent through a private connection stay in private.
